How to play

A snake is a path in the hypercube where no two of its vertices are adjacent unless they are consecutive on the path. Grow snakes as long as you can.

  • Tap a white or pink vertex to extend the nearest snake end to it. If more than one end could reach it, you choose.
  • Tap a snake end to select it; its legal moves light up in blue. Tap them in turn to keep growing.
  • Tap a vertex on a snake to cut the snake there, or to delete it.
  • Tap an empty vertex to start a new snake when a slot is free.
  • Shading shows how many snake vertices a vertex touches, from white (none) to deep red. An amber ring marks vertices some end can still reach.
  • Extend snakes runs an exhaustive search from the current position, growing only the snakes already on the board. It stops at the budget set in Settings and keeps the best it found.
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to pan. In the grid layout, rows are the low bits and columns the high bits, both in Gray-code order, so each row and column is a hypercube of its own.

Longest known snakes: Q2 2, Q3 4, Q4 7, Q5 13, Q6 26, Q7 50, Q8 98. Q9 and Q10 are open.
